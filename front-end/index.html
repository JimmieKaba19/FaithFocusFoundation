About Us

Bringing Hope, Fostering Unity.

"Even so faith, if it hath not works, is dead, being alone."

James 2:17

The Faith Focus Foundation is a community transformation organization dedicated to providing spiritual growth, psychological assistance, and economic empowerment to the needy in Laikipia East. Born from a unique alliance between uniformed services and civilian leaders, our core mission is the successful rehabilitation of street families and the reintegration of ex-inmates, ensuring every vulnerable person has hope and a path toward a better life.

Our Guiding Mission

Our purpose is defined by our motto: Bringing Back Hope to the Needy. We achieve this by providing a pathway for holistic growth, spiritual foundation, psychological healing through dedicated counseling, and practical social-economic initiatives that empower individuals to take control of their future and achieve lasting stability.

Be a Part of the Movement

We are a movement driven by action, and we need dedicated hearts and hands. We invite you to contribute your time and skills in mentorship, administrative support, or community outreach. Your commitment is the most direct way to support someone's journey from vulnerability to self-sufficiency.

Our Unified Collaboration

Our greatest strength is our unique alliance. We are not a traditional NGO, but a unified front where the Kenya Police, Prisons Service, local churches (CFF), and civilian stakeholders (Matatu owners, business community) work as one team. This cross-sector collaboration ensures our efforts have deep institutional support, resource sharing, and lasting community impact.

What We Do

Our Programs: Transforming Lives

Rehabilitation & Social Support

Running counselling and reformation sessions for street families, leading to successful resettlement and reintegration into society.

Legal & Identity Assistance

Organizing initiatives to pay for and secure National ID cards and Birth Certificates for street family members who lack formal identification.

Reintegration for Formerly Incarcerated

Providing hands-on assistance to released individuals by helping them bond with the community, opening small businesses, and securing employment opportunities.

Community & Spiritual Unity

Organizing large community-wide conferences and thanksgiving services to foster spiritual growth and bridge the gap between uniformed services (Police, Prisons, AP) and the civilian community.

Welfare & Emergency Aid

Organizing community celebration events (e.g., Christmas parties for inmates and street families) and covering the costs of severe medical treatment (e.g., skin transplants) for vulnerable individuals.

Economic Empowerment

Developing and seeking capital for income-generating projects focused on environmental cleanup (e.g., the Glass Project, utilizing garbage to create products).

Combating the cyclical challenges of economic marginalization by providing practical support, mentorship, and immediate economic opportunities to individuals transitioning from correctional facilities back into society, fostering positive community bonds to prevent recidivism.
